About AMG Courrier

Moving what matters — with precision, integrity, and relentless focus on on-time performance.

Our Story

From Regional Courier to National Logistics Partner

Founded in 2012 in Chicago, AMG Courrier Service began as a focused same-day courier for the legal and healthcare sectors. Demand for reliability quickly expanded our reach.

Today we operate a multi-modal network spanning ground, air, and specialized logistics, serving more than 12,000 business clients across the United States and 85+ international destinations.

Our growth has always been guided by one principle: treat every package as if it carries someone’s most important deadline.
